Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India & 11th President

Dr. Kalam dedicated his entire life to scientific advancement, education, and inspiring the youth of India.

Milestones & Legacy

  • 1931 - Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, overcoming poverty by selling newspapers to support his family.
  • 1960 - Graduates in Aeronautical Engineering and joins India's Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO).
  • 1980 - Successfully leads the team that launches India's first satellite launch vehicle (SLV-III), placing the Rohini satellite in orbit.
  • 2002 - Elected as the 11th President of India, affectionately earning the title "The People's President".
  • 2015 - Passes away while doing what he loved most—delivering a lecture to students—leaving a legacy of youth empowerment.
